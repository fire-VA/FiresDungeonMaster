## 0.1.202

### New
- Admins can pick the dungeon for entrances placed with FiresAdminPrefabs (from the entrance's menu) and for Entry Doors; a fully generated entrance is reset to empty first. World locations are never touched.
- Morkhalla and The Hole added to the picks; picks are the teleport dungeons only.
### Changed
- A dungeon room placed on the ground no longer makes an Entry Door or box (the "Entrance makes its box" setting is removed).
- Clearing a zone refuses if the zone holds any other location.
- Note: the server setting "Clear zone on last entrance" (off by default) deletes the objects in a zone when its last entrance is removed.
- No stock locations load at login; a dungeon type copies its tuning when it generates.
- Quieter log.
### Fixed
- Rooms rebuilt later keep their dungeon.
- A picked dungeon no longer has the sky floor stuck inside it.
